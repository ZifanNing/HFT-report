# LOB ML baseline: top-of-book features to mid-price direction

Not a single paper; a baseline in the spirit of the classical LOB-prediction literature
(Kercheval & Zhang 2015 "Modelling high-frequency limit order book dynamics with SVMs";
Ntakaris et al. 2018 FI-2010 benchmark; Sirignano & Cont 2019 "Universal features of price formation").
Purpose: exercise the `Model` / `DatasetBuilder` / time-split path end to end and establish the
number every later model must beat.

## 1. 问题与设定
资产 / 频率 / 数据：
- Assets: `synthetic_dev/SYN` (seeded random-walk LOB, 200k canonical events -> ~43k top-of-book changes),
  and `binance_btcusdt_20260918/BTCUSDT` (one recorded day of Binance depth@100ms + trades, 2.24M deltas ->
  ~35.6k top-of-book rows in 5 hole-free segments; §6 was written against the 8-minute smoke subset).
- Sampling: one row per top-of-book change (`hft.features.book_series.top_of_book`). On Binance a "row"
  is one 100 ms diff batch, so "1 event" ≈ 100 ms and "50 events" ≈ 5 s.
- Target: sign of future mid change at horizons {1, 5, 10, 50} rows and {1 s, 5 s}; three classes
  (down / flat / up, threshold 0 ticks). Continuous target: mid change in ticks at the same horizons.

## 2. 模型 / 策略公式
Features at row i (all backward-looking, `hft/features/basic.py`):
spread (ticks), top imbalance (b−a)/(b+a), 5-level depth imbalance, microprice − mid (ticks),
lagged mid returns over 1/5/10 rows, rolling realized vol of 1-row mid changes (window 50),
last trade sign, seconds since previous row, plus OFI over 1 and 10 rows (`hft.features.ofi.OFI`, added after
the §6 predictions were written).

Classifier: multinomial logistic regression on standardized features, `class_weight="balanced"`, C = 1.
Regressor: Ridge (α = 1) on standardized features -> continuous mid change; predicted score evaluated
with `hft.eval.signal_eval` (Spearman IC, IC t-stat over 10 time buckets, hit rate).

## 3. 原文参数
Kercheval & Zhang: 134 hand-crafted features (levels, time-insensitive & time-sensitive), RBF SVM,
horizon 5–20 events, threshold on mid move. FI-2010: 144 features, horizons 10–100 events, ridge / SLFN.
This baseline keeps 10 features and a linear model.

## 4. 原文声称的结果
Kercheval & Zhang report ~70–80 % up/down accuracy on 5 NASDAQ stocks (day-level fits);
FI-2010 linear baselines reach F1 ≈ 0.4–0.5 at 10-event horizon, non-linear models ≈ 0.6–0.8.
Widely noted caveat: much of the reported accuracy comes from the flat class and short-horizon
mean reversion of the spread, not exploitable directional edge.

## 5. 复现所需的平台能力
- 保真度：L1 (top of book + 5-level depth) suffices; no simulation needed.
- 接口层：Signal (batch), Model (fit/predict/save/load), `DatasetBuilder` + `time_split` with embargo;
  labels from `hft.eval.labels.make_labels`; report via `build_signal_report`.

## 6. 复现前的预期
Written before running `evaluate.py`. `binance_btcusdt_smoke` below means the ~8-minute conversion that
carried that name at the time; the dataset now called `binance_btcusdt_smoke` is a 298.6 s view of the
full day (see results.md). The predictions are left as written.

1. `synthetic_dev`: mid follows a seeded random walk with no order-flow feedback, so every feature is
   independent of future mid changes. Expect classifier accuracy ≈ majority-class accuracy, up/down hit
   rate ≈ 0.50 ± noise, |IC| < 0.02 and IC t-stat within ±2 at all horizons. Any material edge here is
   a lookahead bug, not a result.
2. `binance_btcusdt_smoke`: real order flow. Expect top imbalance and microprice − mid to carry a small
   positive edge at 1–5 row horizons (hit rate 0.52–0.58, IC 0.03–0.10), decaying toward zero by 50 rows
   and 5 s. Lagged returns likely show mild mean reversion at 1 row (bid-ask bounce) and nothing beyond.
3. Because Binance rows are 100 ms batches with ~900 deltas each, "1 event" is much coarser than in the
   papers, so the strongest microstructure effects (queue depletion at 1 delta) are partly averaged away;
   the edge may be weaker than the literature's 1-event numbers.
4. Sample size: ~3.9k Binance rows -> ~1.1k test rows -> ±1.5 pp standard error on hit rate. Anything
   under ~0.53 is indistinguishable from chance; IC t-stat over 10 buckets will be noisy.
5. `class_weight="balanced"` will pull accuracy below the majority baseline when the flat class dominates
   (short horizons); macro-F1 and up/down hit rate are the meaningful comparisons there.
6. Literature-level accuracy (70 %+) will not be reproduced: no deep book levels, linear model, tiny sample.
