# Notes — LOB ML baseline: top-of-book features to mid-price direction

## Predictions (paper.md §6) vs outcome

| # | Prediction | Held? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | synthetic: no edge at any horizon | **No at 1–10 ev**, yes at 50 ev / 1 s / 5 s. Hit 0.625, IC 0.088 (t = 7.3) at 1 ev, decaying to IC 0.020 (t = 0.7) at 50 ev. Cause is mechanical, not lookahead: the generator's trades consume the best level, so a thin side of the touch predicts which side vanishes next. Non-lookahead tests (perturb later rows) pass for features and labels; a leak would not decay with horizon. |
| 2 | Binance: small positive edge at 1–5 ev (hit 0.52–0.58, IC 0.03–0.10), decaying by 50 ev / 5 s | Direction held, magnitude badly underestimated (full day): hit 0.98 / 0.90 / 0.85 at 1 / 5 / 10 ev, 0.59 at 50 ev; IC 0.33–0.43 then 0.24 (t = 15–18, then 8). Same reason as above, much stronger on real data because Binance top levels routinely shrink to dust (min bid size 1e-5 BTC) before disappearing. |
| 3 | 100 ms batching weakens 1-event effects | Not observed; batching did not dilute the depletion signal. |
| 4 | Sample too small for stable IC_t | Yes on the original 8-min smoke set (10 ev IC_t 5.6 vs 0.1 across segments); the full day gives IC_t 15–18 at ≤ 10 ev. |
| 5 | Balanced weights drop accuracy below majority at flat-heavy horizons | Yes at every horizon with flat ≥ 0.5. |
| 6 | Literature accuracy not reproduced | Yes for 3-class accuracy (0.3–0.6); the up/down-only hit rate exceeds it, for the mechanical reason above. |

Top imbalance is the dominant feature everywhere; microprice − mid and `ofi_e1` add a little at 1–10 ev
(OFI: +1 pp hit, +0.01 IC vs the run without it); depth imbalance enters with the opposite sign on Binance
at 1 ev (conditional on top imbalance, deep-side depth flags a level about to be eaten). `ofi_e10`, lagged
returns, volatility, trade sign and inter-arrival time carry ~nothing. OFI landed after §6 was written and
after the first run; it was added to the feature list without changing the predictions.

## Pitfalls found

- First recording: the replayed Binance book disagreed with the next recorded snapshot at 4 of 17 resyncs
  (first one $143.6 at 14:43:39.8). Reported; the Data Engineer fixed the recorder and reconverted the day.
  The reconverted set replays exactly and marks 4 real data holes with an empty snapshot. `DatasetBuilder`
  now splits the series where the row gap exceeds `max_gap_ns` (5 s) and builds features and labels per
  segment, so no label or lag crosses a hole (5 segments, 484 rows dropped at segment edges / null windows).
- Ridge R² was negative on the old 8-min smoke split (heavy-tailed labels, a few hundred training rows);
  on the full day it is +0.04 to +0.09 with no train/test gap.
- `time_split` embargo uses the max label horizon in rows, computed from the data for time horizons
  (`DatasetBuilder.required_embargo`): 201 rows on synthetic (5 s at ~40 rows/s), 50 on Binance.
- `hft lint` (default paths) fails on `hft/features/book_series.py` importing `hft.sim.replayer`; not mine.

## What a follow-up should do

- Rebuild the Binance recording with a properly synced first snapshot, then rerun; add ≥ 1 h of data.
- Evaluate on a "tradeable" label: mid change beyond half a spread, or fill-aware markout, rather than the sign
  of the next half-tick.
- OFI is in; a proper incremental test (OFI alone vs imbalance alone vs both) needs more data than 1k rows.
- `walk_forward` is implemented but unused here; with more data report per-fold IC instead of one split.
