# Notes — Avellaneda & Stoikov (2008)

## Deviations from the paper and why

| Paper | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fills are Poisson with intensity `A exp(-k delta)` | Fills come from replayed order flow through `Naive` / `ProbQueue` | The point of the platform: the queue models bracket the fill answer; `A` is unused |
| Dimensionless price, `T = 1`, `sigma = 2`, `gamma = 0.1`, `k = 1.5` | Prices in ticks, time in seconds, inventory in clips of `order_size`; `k` per tick, `sigma` in ticks/sqrt(s) | Ticks are the natural unit of a 0.01-tick book. Only `gamma * sigma^2 * (T - t)` (ticks of skew per clip) transfers, so `gamma` is chosen per dataset: `2e-4` on SYN (sigma ~2.6), `5e-10` on BTC (sigma ~850). `k = 1.5`/tick gives a spread floor `(2/gamma) ln(1+gamma/k) ~ 1.33` ticks, roughly the market spread |
| Continuous prices | Quotes rounded to the nearest tick, then capped so they never cross: `touch_mode = join` (<= best bid / >= best ask), `improve` (may sit inside the spread), `behind` (best +- 1 tick) | Exchange constraint; `join` is the default so the AS quote can reach the touch when the model spread is narrow |
| Unbounded inventory | `max_inventory` clips; a side is not quoted if `position + open exposure on that side + size` would breach it | Risk control for the sim; the paper's exponential utility makes large `q` rare but not impossible |
| Known constant `sigma` | Rolling realised variance of the mid sampled every `sigma_sample_ms` over `sigma_window_s`, floored at `sigma_floor`, `sigma_init` until warm | No true sigma on real data; on SYN the estimate settles at ~2.6 ticks/sqrt(s) within a few seconds |
| Quotes updated every `dt = 0.005` | Requote timer every `requote_ms` (100 ms); optional requote on every touch change | Binance diff batches arrive as runs of deltas with equal `ts_event`; requoting per delta would place orders on half-applied batches. On SYN events are ~10 ms apart so quotes are stale for up to 10 events |
| Orders always of size 1 | `order_size` in units, rounded to the lot | Same idea |
| No distance cap | `max_distance_ticks` (20 SYN, 50 BTC): a quote farther than this from the touch is not posted | Avoids parking orders 100s of ticks away when inventory skew is large; they would never fill except on a crash and inflate order counts |

## Implementation notes

- `QuoterBase` owns the order management: one live order per side, replace only when the
  rounded price changes, cancel the old order before posting the new one, and track
  cancel-in-flight ids so a cancel is never sent twice. `AvellanedaStoikov` and
  `SymmetricQuoter` only differ in `quotes()`.
- `submit`/`cancel` are asynchronous with entry latency. With latency an order can arrive
  after the book moved through its price; the exchange then fills it as a **taker**. That is
  the mechanism behind the latency degradation and is visible in `fills.maker_share`.
- Metrics logged at each requote: `inventory` (clips), `as_sigma`, `as_tau_s`,
  `as_r_offset_ticks` (`r - mid`), `as_spread_ticks`; `sym_spread_ticks` for the baseline.
- The reservation offset is `-q * gamma * sigma^2 * tau` so the test asserts
  `sign(r - mid) = -sign(q)` whenever `q != 0` and `tau > 0`.

## Pitfalls hit

- Earlier in the wave `hft lint` (default paths) flagged `hft/features/book_series.py` for its
  sanctioned `hft.sim.replayer` import; that is fixed upstream and `hft lint` is clean.
- Matching semantics changed mid-wave (a resting order is maker-filled on a cross only when
  the opposite best *moves* through it; a crossing limit order that exhausts visible depth
  fills the remainder as taker `partial_visibility`; ProbQueue fills are whole lots). All
  numbers in `results.md` were produced after those changes and after the Binance
  reconversion.
- `sigma_floor = 0` lets the spread collapse to the ln-term with no skew when the mid has not
  moved inside the window (first seconds of a run); use a floor.
- A finite horizon clamps `tau` to 0 after `horizon_s`; then AS degenerates to a symmetric
  quoter at `(2/gamma) ln(1+gamma/k)` with no inventory skew. `horizon_s` must cover the
  session (1400 s for SYN, 4700 s for BTC) or `rolling_horizon: true` must be used.
- `binance_btcusdt_20260918` has 4 data holes (705 s total) marked by an empty
  `BookSnapshot`, after which `book.is_valid` is False until the resync snapshot. `QuoterBase`
  cancels every resting order as soon as `on_book` sees an invalid book and never quotes on
  one; the sigma sampler skips invalid books, so the first sample after a resync starts a new
  (mid, ts) pair and the jump across the hole enters the realised variance once. Holes appear
  as flat segments in the inventory / PnL curves.
