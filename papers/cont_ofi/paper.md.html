# Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (2014) — The price impact of order book events

Journal of Financial Econometrics 12(1), 47–88.

## 1. 问题与设定

Does a single linear variable built from top-of-book order flow explain short-horizon
price changes? The paper defines order flow imbalance (OFI) from best bid/ask price and
size changes only, and shows that mid-price changes over fixed time intervals are linear in
OFI with a slope that is inversely proportional to market depth.

Data: 50 US stocks from the S&P 500 sample, April 2010, NASDAQ TotalView-ITCH level-2
(best bid/ask and sizes), 9:30–16:00. Time intervals of 10 s (primary), also 1 s to
several minutes for robustness; half-hour sub-periods for the depth regression.

## 2. 模型 / 策略公式

For consecutive best-quote states `n-1 -> n` with bid price/size `(P^B, q^B)` and ask
`(P^A, q^A)`:

```
e_n = 1{P^B_n >= P^B_{n-1}} q^B_n - 1{P^B_n <= P^B_{n-1}} q^B_{n-1}
    - 1{P^A_n <= P^A_{n-1}} q^A_n + 1{P^A_n >= P^A_{n-1}} q^A_{n-1}
```

`e_n` is the net change in bid-side supply minus ask-side supply at the touch: a bid size
increase, a bid price increase, an ask size decrease or an ask price increase all count as
positive flow. OFI over an interval `(t_{k-1}, t_k]` is `OFI_k = sum e_n` over the events in
the interval. The contemporaneous regression is

```
dP_k = alpha + beta * OFI_k + eps_k,      dP_k = (P_mid(t_k) - P_mid(t_{k-1})) / tick
```

with the structural prediction `beta ~= 1 / (2 D)` where `D` is the average depth beyond
the touch (a "stylised" book with constant depth per tick). The paper also shows that
trade imbalance (buy minus sell volume) has weaker explanatory power than OFI and adds
little once OFI is included.

On this platform OFI is used two ways: (a) the paper's contemporaneous fixed-window
regression, and (b) as a causal trailing-window signal (`OFI` over the last `W` events or
last `w` seconds) evaluated against forward mid changes, which is the platform's standard
signal test and is *not* what the paper claims.

## 3. 原文参数

Interval `Delta t = 10 s`; half-hour windows for estimating depth `D`; OFI in shares,
price changes in ticks (cents); OLS per stock per half hour; robustness over 
`Delta t in {1 s, ..., 10 min}` and across 50 stocks with different tick/price ratios.

## 4. 原文声称的结果

- Contemporaneous `R^2` of `dP ~ OFI` averages ~65% across stocks (10 s windows), with
  beta significantly positive in essentially every stock and half hour.
- `R^2` increases with the interval length up to a few minutes and is lowest at the
  finest resolution (1 s); the linear relation holds across time scales.
- `beta` is inversely proportional to depth: `log(beta) ~ -lambda log(D)` with
  `lambda ~ 1` and `R^2 ~ 70-90%` in the cross-section.
- Trade imbalance explains less (R^2 ~ 30-40%) and adds nothing incremental.
- Price impact is linear in OFI (no concavity as often reported for trade volume).

## 6. 复现前的预期

Written before any run.

1. **Contemporaneous regression on BTC: beta > 0 and material R^2, but below the paper's
   65%.** BTC top-of-book queues are large and fragmented and Binance publishes 100 ms diff
   batches, so `e_n` is coarser than ITCH events. Expect R^2 at 1 s of 10–30%, rising with
   the window (5 s, 10 s, 30 s) as in the paper, but with only 7 minutes the 30 s window has
   ~14 points and its R^2 is noise. Only 1–5 s windows are readable.
2. **Contemporaneous regression on `synthetic_dev` should give a clean positive beta
   because of how the generator works, not because of economics.** A buy sweep removes the
   best ask level and the ask price rises, which contributes `+q^A_{n-1}` to `e_n`: positive
   OFI coincides with an upward mid move by construction. Size refills are random and
   uncorrelated with the mid, so they add noise: expect R^2 well below the paper (guess
   20–50%) and a beta of order `1 / (2 * mean level size)` in ticks per unit size.
3. **Trailing-window OFI as a forward predictor should be weak.** On `synthetic_dev` the
   touch is a pure random walk with no state dependence, so IC of any signal at any horizon
   should be ~0 (|IC| < 0.02). On BTC, expect a small positive IC (0.02–0.08) at 1–10
   events and 1 s, decaying with horizon; short-horizon predictability of the microprice
   kind is well documented but the paper does not claim it.
4. **Microprice offset and top imbalance will beat OFI as forward predictors** at the
   shortest horizons on BTC (they encode the current queue state; OFI encodes its change),
   and OFI should correlate with the change in imbalance rather than its level. Expect
   |IC| ordering at 1 event: imbalance ~ microprice > OFI > last-mid-change.
5. **Last-mid-change (naive momentum baseline)** should show slightly negative IC at 1
   event on both datasets (bid-ask bounce / mean reversion of the mid at the tick level),
   near zero on synthetic.
6. **Beta ~ 1/(2D)** cannot be checked in the cross-section (one instrument per dataset);
   we only check its sign and order of magnitude against the average top-of-book depth.
7. Event-horizon labels on BTC are in units of *top-of-book batches* (100 ms), not
   individual events, so "1 event" on BTC is ~100 ms and not comparable to the synthetic
   "1 event" (~10 ms).
